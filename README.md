# Electioneer

## Background

In 2020 I began to be increasingly frustrated regarding the partisan bickering regarding elections and decided that the answer to controversy was an election system that:

1. Allowed individuals to easily register and/or change their registration status online.
2. Was online. Eliminating the need for the majority of mail-in ballots as well as the use of insecure voting drop boxes.
3. Provided auditable results in realtime, and allowing greater transparency to quash any partisan bickering. 
4. Could be stood up easily by small and large jurisdictions and did not require IT support.

The idea percolated for quite sometime, until 2025 presented me the opportunity to think harder, and, once I had taken care of a small 'family emergency', I decided to get off my duff and do something about my ideas. 
The result is **Electioneer**.
